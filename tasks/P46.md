# P46 — Enemy marshal peels toward the player Nexus after the opening

**Builder:** Composer 2.5 · **Bar:** `docs/DESIGN.md` §6 (scripted AI) · **Shipped:** 2026-08-16

**Live:** https://spacepixelrts.pages.dev · **Version:** `0.3.3-wave2`

---

## Problem (from P42)

Scripted AI only AttackMoved the player Nexus when **6+ military were Idle**. Opening Kryos fighters held `Ord.Attack` mid-map and never peeled, so the player could ignore base macro during the opening minute. Wave 2 needs **ruling under threat**.

---

## Changes

| Area | Fix |
|---|---|
| **`stepMarshalPeel()`** | From tick **240**, surviving enemy military (mid-clash `Attack`, `Idle`, orphan `Attack`, or mis-aimed `AttackMove`) peel toward the player Hall with **0.15s stagger** per unit. Orders set via `orderAttackMoveHall` (`tx`, `tz`, `tid` = player Nexus). |
| **`stepAi()`** | Removed **6+ military** gate. From tick 240, any enemy military not already raiding the player Hall is redirected. Barracks trains **Scouts** at Spark when Fighters are epoch-gated; Hall mirrors Scout production once workers ≥ 8. |
| **Train spawn** | Enemy military trained at tick ≥ 240 spawn with AttackMove on the player Hall. |
| **`openingDmgMul`** | After tick **50**, Kryos clash damage tapers so rank soldiers can survive to peel (P41 wreck tableau in the first ~2.5s unchanged). |
| **`main.ts`** | `VERSION = 0.3.3-wave2`. |

Opening clash **hold `Attack` for ticks 0–239**, win/lose, epochs, and pop unchanged.
