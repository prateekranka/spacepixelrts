# P34 — Readable bolts without chrome scrum

**Builder:** Composer 2.5 · **Gap from:** `tasks/P33-critic.md`

## Problem

P33 fat dual-bolts (scale 2.05, velocity-offset twin) plus oversized HP bars turned the opening gap into one white scrum — ranks existed but fire was unreadable.

## Changes

- **Bolt billboard:** scale **1.28**, **one** instance per bolt (removed velocity-offset twin); zero flash, no shadow quad.
- **Atlas `bolt-sting` / `bolt-beam`:** orange/gold sting + cyan beam with **1px** white core — not a 16px white slab.
- **Opening muzzle pop:** clash fighters flash **0.1** (was 0.55) so hull chrome doesn't blow out the gap.
- **HP overlay:** **18×3 px**; show only when `hp < maxHp * 0.92` or selected; never on `Kind.Resource`; opening-clash fighters skip bars unless selected.
- Opening bolt cadence (speed **6.0**, life **1.4**, cooldown **0.32**) and freeze-fire ranks unchanged.

## Verification

`critic/out/p34.png` — p99 **4.2 ms**, probe **0.2.9-wave1**, 58 ents. Two countable wings with orange/gold and cyan bolt pixels bridging the gap; no white box pile. Deployed to https://spacepixelrts.pages.dev
