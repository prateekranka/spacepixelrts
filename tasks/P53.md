# P53 — Foot ellipses + combat-only HP bars

**Builder:** Composer 2.5 · **Bar:** `docs/DESIGN.md` §5.4 #3–4 · **Shipped:** 2026-08-16

**Live:** https://spacepixelrts.pages.dev · **Version:** `0.4.2-wave3`

---

## Gap (from P50)

Selection rings floated at sprite center with HP bars on any damaged unit. DESIGN wants **foot ellipses** and HP only on **selected, damaged, or in combat last 1.2s**. Opening clash must not wallpaper with HP chrome when nothing is selected.

---

## Changes

| Area | Result |
|---|---|
| **Selection** | 1px foot ellipse per selected unit (ally `#48f048`, enemy `#f04848`); projected at ground `y=0.05`, not a center ring through the sprite. Multi-select = one ellipse each. |
| **HP bars** | Shown only when selected **or** `hp < maxHp` **or** `combatT > 0` (1.2s after strike). Ally green / enemy red fill + 1px outline. |
| **Opening** | `tick < 240` with nothing selected → **no HP bars** on the clash (combat linger suppressed until post-opening). |
| **Combat timer** | `combatT` on `Ent`; `markCombat()` on melee/bolt hit for attacker + defender; decays per tick. |

**Not in scope:** P51 sparks, P52 dissolve, opening marshal restage.
