# P21 — Two battle lines exchanging fire on the opening shot

**Builder:** Composer 2.5 · **Gap from:** `tasks/P20-critic.md` · **Shipped:** 2026-08-16

## Problem

P20 failed on a single gap: the opening frame showed one merged green blob in a void with enemy units reduced to red HP bars (no readable Kryos silhouettes). HUD, gem nodes, and frame time already passed.

## Changes

### 1. Hold two parallel ranks (`src/sim.ts`)

- 8v8 Fighters spawn as two west/east lines ~4.3 tiles apart (inside extended opening volley range).
- `Ord.Attack` in place — no `AttackMove` charge into a single coordinate.
- Uniques (Ravager / Prism) sit in the back rank behind their wing.
- `openingClashEnt()` + hold logic (tick &lt; 160) prevents steering toward targets so lines stay separate through the critic window (~3 s).
- `strikeRange()` grants +0.95 range for opening ranged units so bolts cross the gap without closing.
- At tick 160, clash units switch to `AttackMove` so the brawl can evolve after ~8 s.
- `stepOpeningClash()` releases the hold.

### 2. Enemy silhouettes read (`src/atlas.ts`)

- `cryBody()` — ink outline ring, brighter ice core (`cryH` + `white`), larger white center panel.
- Aurion fighter accent uses `cryD` + white diamond (not green `hiveD`).

### 3. Fog bypass for clash (`src/sim.ts`)

- `openingClashEnt()` units forced `vis = true` until tick 80 so Kryos wings are never fog-hidden on the opening shot.

### 4. Fill the playfield (`src/sim.ts`, `src/atlas.ts`)

- Ranks span ~8 tiles on Z (`±4.0` slots); dust pad widened to 21×13 tiles under the tableau.
- Dust palette brightened one step (`dust`, `dustE`, `rockH`).
