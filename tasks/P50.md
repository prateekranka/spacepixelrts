## Single biggest Wave 3 gap

**Combat is still HP-flash-and-bolts, not AoE2's muzzle → travel → impact → corpse read chain (§5.4).**

The Wave 2 arc sells ruling and mixed-arms pressure, but a fresh critic still cannot read the mid fight purely from VFX the way AoE2:DE does — without selecting units or leaning on HP chrome. Today:

- **Muzzle** pixels are suppressed during the opening clash (`shootFlash = clash ? 0.04 : 0.55`); hits lean on white `hitFlash`, not distinct per-civ muzzle pops (Helion beam, Kryos ice burst, Nihiline spore puff — §5.4 #7).
- **Impact** has no landing spark when bolts arrive; travel reads, payoff does not.
- **Death** is static Kryos wreck placement at spawn (`hp = 0`, `corpseT = 4`) plus `kill()` hp-zero — not the mandated **2-frame dissolve → corpse stain 1.5s → gone** (§5.4 #6–7).
- **Selection** rings float at sprite center with HP bars above; DESIGN wants **foot ellipses** and HP only on selected / damaged / **in-combat last 1.2s** (§5.4 #3–4).

Wave 3 must make "who is winning in one second" (§9 #1) come from silhouette + VFX alone — the polish layer on top of the passed match arc.
