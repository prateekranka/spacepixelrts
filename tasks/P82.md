# P82 — SDF unit and building quads (drop baked sprite atlas)

**Builder:** Composer 2.5 · **Version:** `0.7.2-proc` · **Judged:** 2026-08-16

Units, halls, houses, barracks, and uniques no longer sample a CPU-baked sprite atlas. Shape and color are computed in a dedicated fragment shader from per-instance civ, kind, frame, and team tint.

---

## What shipped

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| `src/sprite-sdf.ts` | GLSL SDF silhouettes — three civ recipes (Helion hex/sail, Kryos crystal, Nihiline tendril), role overlays, building variants, corpse/dissolve frames |
| `src/render.ts` | Dual `InstancedMesh`: `sdfMesh` (units/buildings) + `propMesh` (gems/props atlas) |
| `src/atlas.ts` | Slim 128×128 prop atlas only — gems, wrecks, vents; no unit/building bake |
| Per-instance | `iMeta` (kind, civ, frame, sprite size), `iTeam`, `iFlash` |

---

## Not changed

P81 procedural terrain, P80 GPU VFX, opening staging, civ picker, marshal, HUD.
