## Single biggest Wave 4 gap

**The renderer uploads every visible instance with no viewport or frustum culling while `MAX_ENTS` is capped at 384 (not the contract 2048), so today's ~66-ent opening and ~256-ent Playwright stress still measure p99 ~3 ms but the ARCHITECTURE 400-unit landscape-iPad bar has no headroom path.**

**Smallest independently-judgeable fix:** In `Renderer.draw()`, skip `setMatrixAt` for entities whose interpolated `(x, z)` falls outside the camera's world-space AABB (plus one tile margin) and assert via probe that p99 stays flat when 200+ ents are spawned off-screen vs on-screen.
