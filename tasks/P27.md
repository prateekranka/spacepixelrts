# P27 — Hold-fire ranks with readable spacing

**Builder:** Composer 2.5 · **Gap from:** `tasks/P26-critic.md` · **Shipped:** 2026-08-16

## Problem

P26 failed because both wings collapsed into one overlap stack on a void grid. Billboard scale exceeded column pitch; `Ord.Attack` still steered units together; AttackMove-through-center experiments (P23–P25) were retired in DESIGN.md §6.

## Changes

### A. Formation (`src/sim.ts` `spawnScenario`)

- `colPitch = 1.55`, `rowPitch = 1.40`, `gap = 3.60` between rank centers on Z.
- 8 Fighters per side in 2×4 ranks on shared world X, split on Z; `Ord.Attack` with `tx/tz` on enemy rank.
- Uniques at `(cx - 1.35, zHelion|zKryos)`.

### B. Hold-fire freeze (`thinkUnits` + `moveSeparate`)

- For `tick < 240` and `openingClashEnt(e)` with `Ord.Attack`: acquire, `tryStrike` in range, zero velocity, skip `steer` and separation pushes.
- Opening range bonus (+0.95) and tableau damage/cooldown pacing through tick 240.
- Opening clash + nearby gem visibility forced through tick 240.

### C. Readable sprites (`src/render.ts`)

- Infantry scale **1.08 × 1.14** (buildings and 0.85wu gems unchanged).
- HP bars only when sprite drawn (`drawnEntIds`).

### D. Frustum fill (`stampOpeningGround`, `src/main.ts`)

- 28×20 dust pad; rim rocks (pad edge + inner corners outside 8-tile Z fire lane).
- Four gems at `(cx±8, cz±5)`.
- `input.halfH = 5.8`; probe `0.2.2-wave1`.

### E. HUD (`src/hud.ts`)

- `#fps` text: `"${fps} FPS"` beside Idle worker button.
