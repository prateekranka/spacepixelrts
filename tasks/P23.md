# P23 — Packed crossing brawl on the opening shot

**Builder:** Composer 2.5 · **Gap from:** `tasks/P22-critic.md` · **Shipped:** 2026-08-16

## Problem

P22 failed on a single gap: two undersized clumps (~6 vs ~5) stood off across ~63% void. DESIGN §6 wants 8v8 (we ship 12v12) already mixing through each other with visible ranged fire filling the playfield.

## Changes

### 1. Crossing brawl spawn (`src/sim.ts`)

- **12 vs 12 Fighters** plus Ravager / Prism uniques in a tight 10×6 tile box centered on the camera.
- Wings start **2.2 tiles apart** with `Ord.AttackMove` through each other from tick 0 (removed P21 hold-until-tick-160).
- Staggered spawn jitter + negative cooldown offsets so lines do not stack on identical coordinates.
- Opening volley: `strikeRange` bonus kept; ranged cooldown **0.48 s** (Prism **0.62 s**) for ticks &lt; 120; opening damage scaled to **0.62×** so the tableau stays dense at t=3 s.

### 2. Fill the void (`src/sim.ts`, `src/atlas.ts`, `src/render.ts`)

- Dust pad expanded to **29×19** tiles under the camera frustum + 48 nebula wisps (gas/solar/dust scatter).
- Rectangular opening vision + fog reveal for ticks &lt; 200 so fog does not paint the frustum as void.
- Dust palette brightened (`dust`, `dustE`, `rockH`).
- Localized **nebula point cloud** + star bias over the tableau (`src/render.ts`).

### 3. Camera (`src/main.ts`)

- `halfH = 4.6` — brawl fills the shot without shrinking unit sprites.
