# P35 — Round bolts, hide opening HP chrome

**Builder:** Composer 2.5 · **Gap from:** `tasks/P34-critic.md`

## Problem

P34 orange/cyan bolt bars matched HP-bar grammar in the opening gap; every fighter wore a full-width HP strip, eating silhouettes and making the wow shot read as floating UI debris.

## Changes

- **Opening HP off:** `world.tick < 240` → overlay HP **only for selected** ents; no bars on unselected fighters during the wow shot.
- **Bolts are dots:** `drawBolt` sting/beam = ~9px **circle** (gold `PAL.sol`, cyan `PAL.cry`) with 2px white center; billboard scale **0.95**, one instance per bolt.
- **iFlash muzzle:** clash-zone flash **0.04** at `cooldown > 0.26` — small pop, not a white slab.

## Verification

`critic/out/p35.png` — p99 **4.2 ms**, probe **0.2.10-wave1**, 58 ents, selected **0**. Gap shows round gold/cyan sparks; fighters have no HP chrome unless selected; two wings still countable. Deployed to https://spacepixelrts.pages.dev
