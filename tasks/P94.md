# P94 — 2:1 isometric projection

## Problem
Camera was a steep ~60° ortho (`lookAt` offset `+8, y, +10`). Ground read as a top-down square, not an AoE2 isometric place. Buildings were front facades.

## Change
- `src/render.ts` — dimetric camera `ISO_YAW = π/4`, `ISO_PITCH = atan(0.5)`, `ISO_DIST = 40`. Depth-sort instances by `x+z` ascending.
- `src/input.ts` — pan from two `view.pick()` samples (iso-correct).
- `src/sprites.ts` — hall/house/barracks/unique redrawn as iso 3/4 boxes (south + east walls + 2:1 roof diamond). `linePix` rounds to integers (float Bresenham hung page load).
- `src/main.ts` — `VERSION = 0.9.0-iso`, `OPENING_PAN.halfH = 7.2`.

Sim untouched.

## Verify
- `npx tsc --noEmit` clean.
- Live `http://localhost:5173` probe `0.9.0-iso`, in-game FPS 60, critic p99 **3.1 ms**.
- Screenshots: `critic/out/p94.png` (opening), `critic/out/p94-hall.png` (zoom on player hall).
