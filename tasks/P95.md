# P95 — Emissive team color

## Problem
Buildings stamped a loud 4×2 `#FF00FF` facade banner via `drawBanner`. Shader `sprite-sdf.ts` already replaces MAG with `iTeam`, but the banner read as a magenta sticker on the wall face — not faction glow.

## Change
- `src/sprites.ts` — deleted `drawBanner` and all four building calls.
- **Buildings** — ONE tiny MAG emissive per type (shader-tinted to team):
  - Hall: spire orb on roof peak (3 px)
  - House: porch lantern beside door (2 px)
  - Barracks: gate lamp above door (3 px)
  - Unique: staff orb on roof peak (3 px)
- **Units** — ONE MAG emissive per living role (2–4 px, held/mounted):
  - Worker: crate lamp (drill) or visor slit
  - Scout: engine glow
  - Fighter: visor slit
  - Siege: muzzle crystal
  - Ravager: predator eye
  - Prism: focus lens facet (beam stays civ CRY)
  - Shade: glowing hood eye
- Corpse keeps small MAG stain (`circ(..., MAG)`).
- `src/main.ts` — `VERSION = 0.9.1-iso`.

Sim, render camera, terrain untouched.

## Verify
- `npx tsc --noEmit` clean.
- `npm run build` clean.
- Screenshot: `critic/out/p95.png` — no bright magenta facade banner; team identity is small glow points.
