## Single biggest remaining gap vs AoE2:DE

**§9 #2 — orders still feel like dragging stacked sprites, not commanding an army.**

Sim and HUD paths work (box-select, move, train, age-up), but AoE2:DE's command *feel* comes from acknowledgment you do not have yet: formation spread on multi-unit moves (blob walks as one pile), strike/combat audio (hits are visually sold by P51–P53 but **silent**), the §7.4 **idle-worker pulse**, and stronger ground-order feedback beyond a fading flag ring. Until that layer lands, §9 #2 stays below the DE bar even though the Wave 3 VFX chain (§5.4 #5–7) and earned HP chrome (§5.4 #3–4) now pass.
