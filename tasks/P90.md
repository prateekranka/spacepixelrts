# P90-v3 — Connected lit pixel-art roster (validated technique)

## What changed

Replaced GPU SDF primitives with **startup-rasterized sprites** using the `art-reference.ts` technique:

1. **INK silhouette first** — whole connected form (head→torso→legs→weapon) before fill.
2. **Top-left 3-tone light** — Hi upper-left, Mid base, Dk lower-right on every surface.
3. **Dominant weapon/tool** — rifle w/ stock flair + muzzle brake, worker crate overlapping thighs, scout dish, siege tread+cannon, ravager scythe claws, prism lens+beam, shade cloak+eye+dagger.
4. **Buildings as places** — pitched roof (wide eaves), wall plane, short wide inset door on ground, 1px lit window slots, civ spire/motif.
5. **Team color** — magenta `MAG` pixels still drive `paintMag` / shader team tint.

`src/sprites.ts` draws the full roster; `src/sprite-sdf.ts` bakes atlas at boot and samples via instanced shader (60fps-safe, no per-frame raster).

### art-reference fixes (critic round 3)

- **Fighter:** front forearm shoulder→trigger, skin hand on barrel; rifle stock flair + muzzle brake (not uniform pipe).
- **Worker:** hunched 1px, crate overlaps thighs.
- **Hall:** short wide door inset at bottom-center wall (not full-height slit).

## Least confident read

**Scout at default opening zoom** — dish+hull read at 4× but at glance distance the low hull can still collapse into a horizontal smear; dish does not dominate as strongly as fighter rifle or worker crate.

**Siege tread detail** — tread pixels are small at default unit scale; role reads as “chunky tank” but individual tread teeth may be lost until zoomed.

**Nihiline unique (umbra relay)** — orb+tendrils are clear at building scale; at edge of minimap the orb can merge with void wall tone.
