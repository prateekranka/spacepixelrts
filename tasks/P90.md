# P90-v2 — Connected lit anatomy + dominant weapons + real buildings

## What changed

Rewrote unit and building draw paths in `src/sprite-sdf.ts` to fix the five named v1 failures:

1. **Connected anatomy** — `paintTaper` leg columns overlap pelvis/torso; feet reach ground with no floating rectangles (fighter, worker, ravager).
2. **Hands on tools** — bent arm segments + hand circles overlapping weapon/crate edges (fighter rifle grip, worker two-hand crate hold, shade dagger grip).
3. **Dominant weapon/tool** — worker ~11px crate; fighter stock+receiver+barrel+muzzle; scout dish r=7 with bracket; siege solid treads + thick elevated barrel + muzzle brake.
4. **Top-left lighting** — `paintShade` / `paintShadeC` / `shadeRectCol` apply lit (upper-left), shadow (lower-right), and base per material on units and buildings.
5. **Buildings as places** — hall/house: foundation, lit wall plane, roof plane above wall seam, dark door opening, 1px lit window slots; civ shapes roof silhouette (dome / crystal tiers / tattered spire) not a banner logo.

Priority sprites (fighter, worker, siege, scout, hall) verified at 4× zoom via Playwright screenshots in `critic/out/p90-v2-*.png`.

## Verification

- `npx tsc --noEmit` — clean
- `npm run build` — succeeds
- Visual: 4× crops show fighter biped + rifle, scout dish > hull, siege tread+cannon, hall dome+walls+door, worker crate+hands in clash/base shots

## Least confident read

**Scout at default opening zoom** — at 4× the dish bracket and hull read clearly, but the hull is very low and wide; when the camera is not zoomed in, the silhouette can still collapse into a horizontal green smear with a bright bump, and the dish does not dominate as strongly as the fighter rifle or worker crate. Dish scale helps at combat distance but scout remains the role most likely to be misread as a generic speed blob at a glance.
