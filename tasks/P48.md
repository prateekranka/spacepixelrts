# P48 — Enemy marshal sends a fighter wave after the opening

**Builder:** Composer 2.5 · **Bar:** `docs/DESIGN.md` §6 (scripted AI army) · **Shipped:** 2026-08-16

**Live:** https://spacepixelrts.pages.dev · **Version:** `0.3.4-wave2`

## Problem (from P47)

Enemy pressure was a **scout timer** — three Scouts AttackMove on the player Nexus at tick 280. Spark epoch blocked Yard Fighters, so the marshal never fielded a real army. DESIGN §6 expects attack-moving the Nexus when **6+ military** exist.

---

## Changes

| Area | Fix |
|---|---|
| **`stepEnemyMarshal()`** | Tick **240**: enemy instant Orbit (`teams[1].epoch = 1`, `ageT = 0`, no banner). Resource floor **≥ 120 ore / 40 charge** after peel; burst at 240. |
| **`pumpMarshalTraining()`** | Every tick ≥ 240, queue **Fighters** from Yard + Nexus when idle (`tryMarshalTrain`, **1 s** marshal train time). |
| **`tryMarshalTrain()`** | Enemy Fighter production shortcut for the off-screen wave. |
| **`stepAi()`** | Pre-240 scout production unchanged; post-240 fighter pump owns Barracks/Hall queues. Removed instant redirect loop (peel handles commit). |
| **`stepMarshalPeel()`** | Index-based **0.15 s** stagger; newly trained units join peel queue instead of teleporting. |
| **`thinkBuildings()`** | Spawned enemy military after 240 enter peel queue as `Idle` until staggered AttackMove. |
| **`main.ts`** | `VERSION = 0.3.4-wave2`. |

Opening clash **0–239**, win/lose banners, and player pop/housing unchanged.
