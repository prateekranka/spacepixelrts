# P91 — Quiet the ground (calm dust field)

## What changed

Rewrote GLSL in `src/terrain.ts` only. Removed the noisy procedural stamp that drowned sprites.

### Removed (root cause of zigzag / sparkle)

- `qElev()` sin ripples (`rippleL` ~6.28, `rippleS` ~12.57) and `floor(bands * 5.0)` 5-step quantization → repeating zigzag/checker at RTS zoom.
- `dustColor()` high-contrast purple (`hi` 0.58 vs `deep` 0.28), 32px pebble hash (`step(0.82, hash21(floor(world * 32.0)))`), and strong sun-rim (`* 0.55`).
- Pixel snap `floor(world * 32.0)` for elevation/rim sampling → tiling stamp.

### New dust field

- **Near-flat mauve base** — tight palette (`deep` 0.362 → `hi` 0.428); ~7% luminance spread so green Helion infantry and dark hall slabs pop.
- **Large-scale value weather only** — two octaves of `valueNoise` at `world * 0.04` and `world * 0.07`, soft blend, no quantization.
- **Faint sun rim** — offset `(-1.2, -0.9)`, gain `* 0.10` on dust; rock rim toned down to `* 0.18`.
- Continuous world-space sampling (no 32px grid snap).

Rock / Void / Ore / Gas / Solar tile identity and `uTiles` / `uDecals` sampling unchanged. Shader is cheaper (fewer sin/hash ops).

`src/main.ts` — `VERSION` bumped to `0.8.0-art`.

## Verification

- `npx tsc --noEmit` — clean
- `npm run build` — succeeds
- Visual: `critic/out/p91.png` — calm low-contrast purple dust field; green units and dark buildings have readable edges; no purple-white zigzag tiling

## Least confident read

**Rock decal boulders** at gameplay zoom still carry local high-contrast ink edges by design; they are sparse and intentional, not the old full-field stamp.
