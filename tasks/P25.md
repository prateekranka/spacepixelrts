# P25 — Both armies on the camera depth plane

**Builder:** Composer 2.5 · **Gap from:** `tasks/P24-critic.md` · **Shipped:** 2026-08-16

## Problem

P24 failed on a single gap: only a slice of the opening brawl was visible (~5 green, 1 blue, orphan HP bars). Wings were split on **world X**, pushing one army toward the camera and one into the distance past the iso frustum while HP bars still projected on-screen.

## Changes

### 1. Same-depth spawn (`src/sim.ts`)

- Both wings centered on **look-at X** (`MAP * 0.5`); separated on **world Z** (screen left/right): Helion at `cz − 1.8`, Kryos at `cz + 1.8`.
- **8 Fighters per wing**, depth spread along X (≤ 2.2 total) at 0.55 spacing; uniques behind each Z-line at `cx − 1.6`.
- `AttackMove` targets enemy wing Z; opening **march-through** closes across the screen until units cross clash center, then fight mixed at `cz`.
- Opening volley damage **0.42×** and slower cooldown through tick 150 so the tableau stays dense at harness time.

### 2. Camera (`src/main.ts`)

- `halfH = 6.0` (was 4.6) — clash center remains look-at; wider frustum keeps both wings in view.

### 3. Orphan HP bars (`src/render.ts`)

- Overlay HP bars only when the entity sprite was actually drawn (`drawnEntIds`).
