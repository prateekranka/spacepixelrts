# P40 — Opening clash has a readable winner

**Builder:** Composer 2.5 · **Gap from:** `tasks/P39-critic.md`

## Problem

P39 fixed mid-gem gather tableau, but the opening duel still read as symmetric wallpaper — equal ranks, ambient sparks, no casualty skew or hit feedback to answer who is winning in one second (§9 #1).

## Changes

- **Asymmetric opening damage:** Kryos (team 1) takes **×1.15** from opening-clash attackers; Helion (team 0) takes **×0.35** incoming (replaces the old **×0.25** blanket nerf).
- **Corpse linger:** `kill()` on units sets `hp = 0`, `corpseT = 1.5` — death frame 4 + stain visible until slot frees; hash/combat skip `hp <= 0`.
- **Kryos hit flash:** `hitFlash = 0.45` on bolt/melee impact, decays over **0.2 s** (`iFlash` in renderer).
- **Workers:** bob scale **1.55** kept; **2 px yellow gather pip** at feet while `tick < 240`.
