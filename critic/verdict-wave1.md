# Critic verdict — wave1-skirmish

**URL:** https://spacepixelrts.pages.dev  
**Winner:** AoE2:DE  
**Wowed:** No

## Blind comparison (iPad landscape ~1180×820)

Imagine Age of Empires II: Definitive Edition at this size: a Dark Age clash you can read in one glance—villagers, town center, wood and gold piles, unit silhouettes, minimap terrain, command card alive with intent.

Starhold at the same aspect ratio fails that bar. The screenshot is technically running at 60fps with 53 entities in the probe, but what you see is not a commanding RTS. The battlefield is a dark, repetitive tile field with no economic landmarks, no structures, and no terrain story. Six units appear as colored specks with health bars; they do not read as armies, workers, or siege. Combat, if any, is a few sparks—not a skirmish a player could parse or care about.

The UI makes it worse: the bottom command deck is mostly dead black space, "Nothing selected," and empty group slots. The minimap is nearly blank. The top resource strip shows numbers, not a world you are fighting over.

Against AoE2:DE blind, no player picks this screenshot. Smooth frames on an empty-looking field do not pass.

## Single biggest gap

Nothing on screen reads as a commanding RTS battle—six anonymous colored specks on undifferentiated dark tiles while the bottom half is empty black chrome, so a player cannot identify units, terrain, economy, or what to do next the way AoE2:DE does instantly at the same iPad size.
